Welcome to EnzoBiryani

Big flavours. Student-friendly prices.

EnzoBiryani started with one simple idea: great biryani shouldn't be a once-a-month treat. We cook every handi the traditional dum way — layered rice and marinated meat, sealed with dough and slow-cooked for aroma that hits you before the lid even opens.

Whether it's a late-night study session, a team lunch or a Sunday family feast, we have a portion size, a spice level and a price that fits.
