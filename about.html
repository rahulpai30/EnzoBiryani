About EnzoBiryani

From a hostel kitchen experiment to a neighbourhood favourite.

Our story

Born from a late-night craving

EnzoBiryani began in 2021 when two college friends, tired of overpriced and under-spiced biryani near campus, started cooking a single handi for their hostel floor. Word spread. Orders doubled. Within a year the handi became a small kitchen — and the kitchen became EnzoBiryani.

Today we still cook the same way: small batches, whole spices ground in-house, and a flour-dough seal on every handi. No shortcuts, no food colour, no day-old rice.

What drives us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve honest, flavour-packed biryani at prices students and families can enjoy every week, not just on special occasions.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted neighbourhood biryani brand, known for consistency, hygiene and warm hospitality.
